## Illustrative (shown as such)
- Max's resale: buys a pair at $200, sells at $260 ("ILLUSTRATIVE" on the card). Resale can lose money too; the line says he sells
  "for more than he paid", which is the condition for it being an asset, not a promise that resale pays.

## Left out on purpose
- Long-run home price growth: the two main long series disagree (Case-Shiller: close to flat after inflation since 1890; the
  Philadelphia Fed's Historical Housing Prices series: real gains, mostly after WWII and since the late 1990s). So the film
  says "it depends" and states the condition, with no figure.
- Whether a home "is an asset" in an accounting sense: the episode uses the cash-flow meaning (money in vs money out), said in line 2.

## Said plainly
- "Education, not financial advice" in the description and on screen while the index fund is shown.
