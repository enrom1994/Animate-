## Said plainly in the film and the description
- Returns are not guaranteed; 7% is a long-run typical figure, not a promise. "Education, not financial advice."
- Used-car loans carry a higher rate (10.6% vs 7.0%); the payment gap already includes that.
- Both cars lose value; the film compares the payments, not which car is worth more.

## Left out
- Insurance, repairs and fuel: they differ by car and would need their own sources. (A used car often costs more to repair and less to insure; the two roughly cancel in common estimates, but that is not sourced here, so it is not claimed.)
- The new car's value at ten years: sources differ too much.
