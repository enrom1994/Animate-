# Sources (read 15 November 2026)

Main source: Connecticut Public Utilities Regulatory Authority, "What To Do If You Smell Natural Gas",
portal.ct.gov/PURA/Gas-Pipeline-Safety/What-To-Do-If--You-Smell-Natural-Gas (updated March 2026). Supporting:
Minnesota Office of Pipeline Safety, dps.mn.gov/divisions/ops/homeowners; Pacific Gas and Electric,
pge.com/en/outages-and-safety/safety/gas-safety.html.

- Line 1: "Don't flip light switches on or off." CT. "never turn electric switches on or off if you suspect a gas leak." PG&E
- Line 2: "Natural gas has no odor. Gas companies add a harmless chemical called mercaptan to give it its distinctive 'rotten egg' smell." CT. Sparks: "Don't use any electrical equipment or lights that might create a spark in the area of the odor." CT
- Line 3: "Do not turn on or off any electric lights or appliances" MN. "Don't use the doorbell." CT
- Line 4: "Don't smoke or strike any matches." "Don't light any candles." CT
- Line 5: "do not try to locate the source yourself." CT. "Never use a flashlight, match or candle to look for gas leaks." PG&E
- Line 6: "evacuate by getting everyone out of your home or building right away" MN. "leave the premises immediately and tell others to leave." CT
- Line 7: "Don't use a telephone." CT. Cell phones are listed as an ignition source "until you are a safe distance away." PG&E. "Move a safe distance away and then call 911" MN
- Line 8: "Call your gas company or 911 for help." CT
- Line 9: "Don't go back inside your home or building until your local gas company says it is safe." CT. "Stay away until emergency responders have told you it is safe to return" MN
- Line 10: "If the smell is strong or you are unsure, leave the building and then call." CT

Left out on purpose, because the sources disagree:
- Opening windows. Minnesota: "Do not try and open or close the windows". Other guidance commonly says to open them.
- Turning off the gas at the meter. PG&E describes how to; Minnesota: "Do not try to shut off the gas".
Also not used: a specific safe distance (none of the pages read states one), and any statistic (none found).
