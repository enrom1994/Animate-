# Video 4 plan: "Smell gas at home? Do NOT flip a light switch"

Status: script drafted and sourced; nothing built. Follows `../STYLE.md` (rules 1 to 21) and the 13 standing checks in `../REGRESSIONS.md`.

## Shape
126 words, about 38 to 40 s with the Fenrir voice, about 20 shots. Pattern: the wrong instinct stopped by the cross
(the light switch), why (an added smell, and one spark), three more "do nots" (switches and appliances and the
doorbell, flames, hunting for the leak), the fix (everyone out, no phone until away, call), the limit (stay out until
told), and a closing rule that loops back to the first shot: leave first, call second.

## The world
- **Inside:** the kitchen from video 2, extended into a hallway with a light switch, a front door with a doorbell, and stairs.
  Reusing the kitchen keeps the channel's world consistent and saves most of a day of building.
- **Outside, new:** the front of the house on a quiet street: path, gate, pavement, a neighbour's house, a lamp post.
  The far side of the street is where Sam phones from.
- **The smell, new:** wavy green-yellow smell lines that drift from the stove and thicken in a room; the same
  device everywhere a gas smell is shown. A spark is a small white-yellow star at the switch.
- **People:** Sam, the friend (as someone else in the house to get out), and a gas company worker in a new look who arrives at the end.

## Every rule from review applied
- Hands on things: the switch, the doorbell, the door handle and the phone are all reached for with Sam's own arm, side-on.
- Doors: Sam opens the front door by its handle and goes through the opening; nobody passes through anything.
- Interiors are built rooms; the friend is fetched from a real room upstairs, not shown as a head.
- The phone call happens across the street, well away from the house, with the house in the background.
- Closing line: a pause before it, at least 1.3 s after it, no sound tail or whoosh under it.

## Sound (real recordings, Mixkit)
Already in the library: electric switch, door hard shut, stove hum, footsteps, whooshes, thuds. To add: a faint gas hiss,
a doorbell (as the thing Sam does not press), a front door opening, a street ambience, a phone dial. All to be found by id.

## Risks and choices
- **Two points left out** because the sources disagree: opening windows, and turning off the gas at the meter. The film
  says neither, so it never tells a viewer to do something an official source says not to.
- **The "leave first" closing line** matches Connecticut's wording exactly; it is the strongest line to end on and loops to the opening.
- **Emergency number:** the film says "your emergency services", chosen by the user so it works in every country.
- **No explosion is shown.** A spark and the cross tell the danger; a blast would be the biggest image in the film and would read as entertainment.
