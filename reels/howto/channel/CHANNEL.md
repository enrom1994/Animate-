## Channel description (paste into About)
Something has gone wrong. Now what, Sam?

Short cartoon guides to getting out of trouble: what to do, what not to do, and why, in under a minute. Rip currents,
kitchen fires, getting lost, and the everyday problems nobody taught you how to handle.

Every tip comes from an official safety source, listed in each video's description. New Shorts every week.

These videos are general safety information, not professional training or emergency advice. In an emergency, call
your local emergency number.

## Settings
- **Category:** Education. **Audience:** "No, it's not made for kids" (general audience; the topics are safety advice
  for everyone, and a made-for-kids channel loses comments, notifications and personalised ads).
- **Keywords:** how to, survival tips, safety tips, what to do if, life hacks, cartoon explainer, animated shorts, now what sam
- **Altered or synthetic content:** the narration is a synthetic voice and the pictures are cartoons. Cartoon
  animation needs no disclosure under YouTube's rule; do not present the voice as a real person.
- **Playlists to start with:** Water, Outdoors, At home, On the road.

## Every video
- **Title pattern:** the trouble, then the hook. "Caught in a rip current? Do NOT swim to shore" (under 60 characters,
  the situation in the first three words).
- **Description:** two lines on what to do, then "Sources:" with the links from the film's `SOURCES.md`, then
  `#shorts` and two topic tags.
- **Pinned comment:** "What should Sam get stuck in next?"
- **First upload:** `../ripcurrent/ripcurrent.mp4`
  - Title: `Caught in a rip current? Do NOT swim to shore`
  - Description: `A rip current will not pull you under, and it is narrow. Swim sideways, along the beach, until the pull lets go, then swim back in at an angle. Too tired? Relax, face the shore and wave for help.` + sources + `#shorts #ripcurrent #watersafety`
