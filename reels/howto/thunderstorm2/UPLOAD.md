## Description
If you can hear thunder, lightning is close enough to strike you. Do not shelter under a tree, do not lie flat, and crouching does not make you safer. Get into a real building, or a car with a metal roof and the windows up. A tent, a porch or a picnic shelter will not protect you.

Nothing in reach? Get off high ground, away from water and away from fences, and spread out if you are in a group. Stay inside until 30 minutes after the last thunder. If someone is hit, it is safe to touch them: call for help.
