## Description
Never throw water on burning oil: it makes the fire bigger and throws burning oil across the kitchen. Do not carry the pan, and do not use an ordinary fire extinguisher on it. Turn off the burner, slide a lid or a baking sheet over the pan, and leave it covered until the pan is cool.

Fire in the oven? Turn it off and keep the door shut. Flames out of the pan? Get out, close the door behind you and call the fire service.
