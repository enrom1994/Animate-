## Title (45 characters)
Caught in a rip current? Do NOT swim to shore

## Description
A rip current will not pull you under, and it is usually narrow. Swim sideways, along the beach, until the pull lets go, then swim back in at an angle. Too tired? Relax, face the shore and wave for help.

See someone else caught? Do not go in after them. Throw something that floats and get a lifeguard.

Sources:
US National Weather Service, rip current science: https://www.weather.gov/safety/ripcurrent-science
US National Weather Service, surviving a rip current: https://www.weather.gov/safety/ripcurrent-surviving
