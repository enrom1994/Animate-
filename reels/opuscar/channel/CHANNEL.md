# Overpass — YouTube channel kit

**Name:** Overpass
**Handle:** @OverpassFilms (fallbacks: @OverpassMaps, @OverpassOrbit; check availability in YouTube Studio → Customization → Basic info)
**Tagline (banner):** The world, measured from orbit.

Why the name: an "overpass" is the moment a satellite passes over a place and takes the picture. Every film is built on those pictures. It is short, easy to say, and leaves room for any country or topic.

## Files
- `profile.png`: 800×800 profile picture. The real Earth (NASA Blue Marble, public domain; no NASA endorsement implied) centred on the Nile, with a yellow orbit ring and a satellite. Reads as a circle down to 24 px.
- `banner.jpg`: 2560×1440 banner (real Landsat 8, GERD reservoir, Jan 2026). The text sits inside the 1546×423 area every device shows.
- `make.py`: rebuilds both.

## Description (Studio → Customization → Basic info)

Geography and geopolitics, told with real satellite data.

Every film here is built from imagery anyone can check: Landsat, NASA, ESA. We measure what changed — a lake that didn't exist, a city that grew into the desert, a river that moved — and then ask who it matters to and why.

Every number on screen is sourced or measured on the images shown. Sources are in each description.

New film every two weeks.

- **Upload defaults:** licence Standard YouTube; captions language English; the description template below
- **Channel trailer** (for people who haven't subscribed): the GERD film, or a 60 s cut of its cold open
- **Featured video** (for returning subscribers): the latest upload
- **Watermark:** `profile.png`, shown from the end of the video
